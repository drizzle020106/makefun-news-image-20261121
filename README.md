One image summing up the world's news of **Saturday 21 November 2026**:
a dark-navy header naming the day, and a 2×3 grid of six stories, each
with a hand-drawn pictogram, a short title and one-line caption.

## The six stories

1. **Russian missiles land in Poland** — Russia fires 400+ drones and
   110 missiles at Ukraine overnight; two missiles come down in Poland
   and NATO calls it a violation; PM Tusk calls it a deliberate
   provocation. (AP, CNN, Business Standard, France 24, CBS)
2. **AI sell-off hits Wall Street** — the S&P 500 and Dow fall into
   correction territory and the Nasdaq erases all its 2026 gains as AI
   shares plunge; Nvidia drops 14%. (Reuters, BBC, FT, WION)
3. **UK and EU sign post-Brexit deal** — three agreements on food
   exports, carbon markets and youth mobility, signed in Brussels.
   (BBC, RTHK, Dawn)
4. **Houthi missiles target Riyadh** — Saudi Arabia intercepts three
   ballistic missiles early on Saturday, the third night in a row.
   (Al Jazeera, WION)
5. **6.8 quake shakes Peru** — a magnitude 6.8 earthquake in the
   Andean Junín region is felt in Lima; residents rush into the
   streets, no major damage reported. (Reuters, Dunya, The Hindu)
6. **GTA VI smashes sales record** — $3.8bn on its first day, the
   biggest entertainment launch ever. (RTHK, News Media House)

## Files

- `draw.py` — Pillow program that draws `entry.png` (build time only).
- `entry.png` — the image, 1024×1024 PNG.
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entry program: copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.
